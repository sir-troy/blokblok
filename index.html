How to play BlokBlok

BlokBlok is a free falling-block puzzle game that runs in your browser. Pieces made of four squares drop into a 10-wide well. Move and turn them so they fit together, fill complete rows to clear them, and don't let the stack reach the top. The more rows you clear, the faster it gets.

Controls

Keyboard

  • ← → move left and right
  • ↑ or X rotate · Z rotate back
  • ↓ soft drop · Space hard drop
  • C hold · P pause · R restart
  • M music · T next theme

Phone and tablet

  • Use the buttons under the board to move, rotate, drop and hold.
  • Hold a move button to keep sliding the piece.
  • ❚❚ pauses the game.
  • Turn your sound on for the music and effects.

Scoring

1 row (Single)100 × level
2 rows (Double)300 × level
3 rows (Triple)500 × level
4 rows at once (BlokBlok!)800 × level
Two BlokBloks in a row (Back-to-back)× 1.5
Combo: clearing rows with pieces in a row+50 × combo × level
Soft drop / hard drop1 / 2 points per row

You go up one level every 10 rows. Each level makes the pieces fall faster and multiplies your points.

Features

  • Hold: save a piece for later and swap it in when it fits better.
  • Next preview: see the next three pieces so you can plan ahead.
  • Ghost piece: a faint outline shows where the piece will land. You can switch it off in Settings.
  • 20 themes: from Neon Night and Pocket 89 to Sakura, Lava Core, Blueprint and Fiesta Pinoy. Turn on "New theme every level up" for a change of scenery as you climb.
  • Chiptune music: several tracks, including the public-domain Russian folk song Korobeiniki, with separate music and sound-effect volumes.
  • Best score: saved in your browser, so you can try to beat it next time.

Tips for higher scores

  • Keep the stack flat and low. Tall towers and deep holes end games quickly.
  • Leave one column on the side open and save the long straight piece for it to clear four rows at once.
  • Use Hold when the current piece doesn't fit anywhere nice.
  • Watch the next pieces, not just the one that's falling.
  • Hard drop with Space once you're sure, to save time and earn extra points.

Questions

Is BlokBlok free?

Yes. There's nothing to buy, no sign-up and nothing to download.

Does it save my progress?

Your best score and settings are saved in this browser. A game in progress isn't saved if you close the page.

Why is there no sound?

Browsers only allow sound after you tap or press a key. Press Start, and check that music is on (the ♪ button or M) and your device isn't muted.

Can I play offline?

Once the page has loaded, the game keeps working without an internet connection.
