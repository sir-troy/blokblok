# BlokBlok

A free falling-block puzzle game in your browser.

**Live:** https://blokblok.useful-indeed.com

BlokBlok is a colourful falling-block puzzle game. Move and rotate the pieces as they fall, fill complete rows to clear them, and keep the stack from reaching the top. It has a hold slot, a ghost piece, combos, 20 themes, chiptune music, and works with a keyboard or touch controls.

## How to use

1. Press Start. Pieces fall from the top of the board.
2. Move with the arrow keys (or the on-screen buttons), rotate with Up or X, and press Space to drop a piece instantly.
3. Fill a whole row to clear it. Clear four rows at once for a BlokBlok and the most points.
4. Press C to hold a piece for later, P to pause, M for music and T to switch themes.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your best score and your settings (theme, music, volume and effects) are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device.
